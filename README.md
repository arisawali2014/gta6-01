**Vice Harbor** — a complete top-down open-world crime game that runs in the browser. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot or by car: steal parked cars, weave through traffic, dodge pedestrians, and outrun a five-star wanted system with police who actually pursue, box you in, and bust you. Fight with fists or a pistol, and run a three-mission story for fixer Marisol Vega — steal a marked coupe, collect her packages under pressure, then hold out at the marina while the heat peaks. Full HUD with a live minimap, cash and health, save & continue via localStorage, and sound synthesized entirely in code. No build step, no image or audio assets: every building, palm, wave, engine note and siren is drawn and sounded by the game's own JavaScript.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts --port 8080
# then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
```
